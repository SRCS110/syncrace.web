How we work

We're not trying to
sell you a subscription.

Most software you're offered today is designed to charge you a little bit forever. It gets redesigned when nothing was wrong with it, it collects more about your customers than it needs, and if you ever stop paying, you lose the thing you helped build.

We build the other way round.

A hotel group needed their numbers each morning without an hour of exporting. A freelancer needed to see their clients and invoices without paying monthly for a sales tool built for a team of forty. Both are real, both are running, and both belong to the people who asked for them.

You own it

The files, the domain, the accounts. Everything is in your name from the start, so leaving is always an option you don't have to think about.

It loads fast

Clean code, small pages, and no tracking scripts running in the background. Your customers get where they're going quickly, on any phone.

We make things ourselves

Design, photography, and the odd game built for the pleasure of it. The habit of making things by hand is what keeps the client work good.

You talk to the person building it

No account manager, no ticket system, no sales call before anyone will answer a question. Just email, and a reply from whoever is doing the work.
