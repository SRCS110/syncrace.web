How we work

Pay us monthly if you like.
Just never because you're stuck.

Most software you're offered is built so that leaving costs you everything. Stop paying and the site goes dark, the data is hostage, and the thing you helped build was never really yours. The monthly fee isn't buying service — it's buying back your own work.

We do sell a monthly plan. It just isn't that.

Ours covers actual work: updates, backups, changes, and someone keeping an eye on things. It's optional, month to month, and if you cancel, nothing switches off. The site was already yours. Some clients take the code on launch day and never speak to us again, and that's a good outcome too.

A hotel group needed their numbers each morning without an hour of exporting. A freelancer needed to see their clients and invoices without a sales tool built for a team of forty. Both are real, both are running, and both belong to the people who asked for them.

It loads fast

Clean code, small pages, and no tracking scripts running in the background. Your customers get where they're going quickly, on any phone.

You talk to the person building it

No account manager, no ticket system, no sales call before anyone will answer a question. Just email, and a reply from whoever is doing the work — retainer or no retainer.
