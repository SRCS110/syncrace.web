2. What we collect

When you use the contact form

  • Your name and email address (required).
  • Your phone number and a budget range, if you choose to add them.
  • The topic you pick and the message you write.

A hidden anti-spam field is also sent. Real visitors never see or fill it in.

When you email us directly

Your email address, your name if included, and whatever you write.

When you visit any page

Like every website, the server that delivers pages receives technical request information: your IP address, browser type, the page requested and the time. Our hosting provider records this in its logs to run and protect the service. We do not use it to identify or profile visitors.

If you accept Analytics

Google Analytics records which pages you view, how you arrived, your device and browser type, and your approximate location (city level), linked to a random identifier stored in a cookie. It does not receive your name or email. If you decline, or never answer, it does not load at all.

Fonts

The site's typefaces are served by Google Fonts. When a page loads, your browser requests them from Google, which receives your IP address and browser details. Google Fonts does not set cookies.

We do not use advertising pixels or social media embeds on this site.

4. Who processes it for us

These are the services actually used to run this site:

  • Vercel hosts the website and runs the small server function that receives the contact form. It keeps request logs as described above.
  • Resend delivers contact form messages to our inbox by email.
  • Google Analytics (Google LLC) measures site usage, only if you accept Analytics. Google's advertising features are switched off.
  • Google Fonts (Google LLC) serves the typefaces used on every page.
  • Cloudflare provides the domain name service (DNS) for srcs.online.
  • [CLIENT TO SUPPLY: email provider that hosts the hello@srcs.online inbox] stores the messages we receive and send.

Each provider handles data under its own terms and privacy policy. We only give them what they need to do their job.
