Questions

Good to know

Why do you host the websites and apps you build?

So one team is responsible for keeping them fast, secure and working. You don't have to deal with hosting accounts, updates or outages.

What does the website build fee depend on?

Mostly the number of pages and features such as booking, forms or a directory. You get a fixed price in writing before we start.

How does hourly billing work?

We estimate the hours before we start. If a job looks like it will run over, we tell you before going further.

Is Google Ads spend included?

No. Ad spend is paid directly to Google on your own card. Our $125/hour covers setting up and managing the campaigns.
