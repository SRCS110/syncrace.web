Questions

Good to know

Can you guarantee a top spot on Maps?

No one honestly can. Google decides rankings based on relevance, distance and prominence. A complete, accurate, active profile with steady reviews gives you the best chance.

Who owns the profile?

You do. It's set up under your Google account, and we're added as a manager you can remove at any time.

Do I need a storefront?

No. Service-area businesses can have a profile that shows the area you serve without displaying your address.
