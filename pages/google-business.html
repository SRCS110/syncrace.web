Questions

Good to know

Can you guarantee a top spot on Google?

No one honestly can. Maps rankings depend on relevance, distance and prominence, and ad positions on your bids and ad quality. A complete, active profile and well-built campaigns give you the best chance.

How is this billed?

At $125 an hour, with an estimate before we start. Ad spend goes directly to Google on your own card. See pricing.

How much should I spend on ads?

It depends on your area, your industry and what a customer is worth to you. We'll suggest a starting budget to test, and you set the limit.

Who owns the profile and ads account?

You do. Both are set up under your Google account, and we're added as a manager you can remove at any time.

Can you fix what someone else set up?

Yes. We can recover a profile, and audit an ads account, fixing the tracking first and then the campaigns.
