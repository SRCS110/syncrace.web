How the pieces connect

Each fold leads to the next.

  • A flyer carries a QR code to the right page of your website, not just the home page.
  • Your website shows the same name, hours and phone number as your Google Business Profile, so Google trusts both.
  • Your Google profile links to booking or ordering, and its reviews and photos appear where customers are already searching.
  • Google Ads send people to pages built for the ad, with tracking that tells you which calls and forms came from them.
  • A small app takes the bookings, requests or orders that all of this brings in, and keeps them in one place.
Questions

Good to know

Do I have to buy everything?

No. Start with whichever piece matters most. Many clients begin with a Google Business Profile or a website and add the rest later.

Who hosts my website or app?

We do. Every website and app we build is hosted and managed by us for a monthly fee, so one team is responsible for keeping it fast, secure and working.

How is pricing handled?

Websites have a one-time build fee plus monthly hosting and management. Apps are quoted per project. Google Business Profile, Google Ads and print work are billed hourly, with an estimate before we start. See pricing.

Whose name are my Google accounts in?

Yours. Your Google Business Profile and Google Ads account are set up under your Google account, and we work inside them as a manager.

Can you work with what I already have?

Yes. We can take over an existing website, claim a profile someone else set up, or clean up an ads account that isn't tracking properly.

Not sure where to start?

Tell us about your business and what you want more of: calls, bookings, foot traffic or time back. We'll suggest the first fold.
