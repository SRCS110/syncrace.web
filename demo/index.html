Our story

One loaf at a time, since the first alarm.

Fern & Fold started in a borrowed kitchen with one starter and a lot of early mornings. Every loaf is still shaped by hand and given two days to ferment.

Flour from a mill an hour north, eggs from down the road, and coffee from a roaster who visits every Tuesday.
