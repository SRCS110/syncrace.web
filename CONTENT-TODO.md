# Content to supply

Legal pages ship with `[CLIENT TO SUPPLY]` markers until these are filled in.
Have your own counsel review the privacy policy and terms before relying on them.

## pages/privacy.html
- Registered legal entity name
- Business mailing address
- Email provider that hosts the hello@srcs.online inbox
- Retention period for enquiry emails
- GA4 data retention setting (2 or 14 months, in Google Analytics > Admin > Data retention)
- Response window for privacy requests
- Effective date, once reviewed

## pages/terms.html
- Registered legal entity name
- Business mailing address
- Governing law and jurisdiction
- Effective date, once reviewed

## pages/accessibility.html
- Target date for fixing the known limitations
- Response window for accessibility feedback
